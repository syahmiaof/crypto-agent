# CRYPTO AI — MISSION CONTROL

## Platform

web

## Stack

User-requested Next.js App Router, strict TypeScript, Tailwind, Radix, Zustand, Lightweight Charts, Recharts, Vitest and Playwright.

## Users and purpose

Professional traders evaluating a simulated multi-agent research, decision and paper-trading workspace. Ten connected routes; review signals, enforce risk limits, inspect research disagreement, and track a coherent virtual portfolio.

## Constraints

Frontend only. No exchange credentials, external LLM inference, real trades, verified performance claims, or production backend. All data is deterministic and visibly simulated. Initial account capital is 10,000 USDT. User explicitly authorized autonomous implementation and delegated design decisions within the detailed brief; no additional interview needed.

## Brand commitments

Institutional terminal, dark restrained surfaces, mint for positive state, red for negative state, Geist typography. No decorative imagery, neon or generic marketing content.

## Accessibility

Keyboard navigation, managed dialog focus, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, six responsive viewport targets.
