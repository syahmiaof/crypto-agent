# Mission Control design system

Operate mode. Institutional research desk with a dense market tape, calm navigation and asymmetric chart/evidence workspace. User brief is the visual authority.

Tokens: background #080C12; sidebar #0D131B; panel #111923; secondary #17212C; border #263340; text #EFF4F8; secondary text #96A6B5; accent #36D6A5; loss #F0717B; warning #E9BC66; info #7EA9E8.

Geist for interface, Geist Mono for prices and measurements. 28px page titles, 17px section titles, 13px body and table. 228px navigation, 64px topbar, 24px workspace padding, 16px grid gaps, 10px panels, 6px controls. Thin borders; no decorative shadows or gradients. Mint square mark with orbit motif establishes identity.

Dashboard: compact six-value ledger; 2:1 chart and evidence rail; signals and agent roster; bottom market pulse. Other routes retain the visual vocabulary with task-specific compositions. Mobile uses a dialog navigation drawer, two-column metrics, stacked evidence rail and contained scrolling tables.
